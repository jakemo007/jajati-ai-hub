> **Short mein:** AI se answer copy karoge to exam mein bhool jaaoge. **AI se answer nahi, sawal lo:** real-life example maango, aur practice sawal jinke answers sabse last mein hon.

## Pehle tumhara test

Rs 100, 10% compound interest, 2 saal: 120 ya 121? Pehle khud socho. Answer neeche notebook wale hisse mein hai.

## 2 tricks

1. **Example:** "Ise real-life example se samjhao."
2. **Practice:** "3 practice sawal do, answers sabse last mein."

Poora prompt (ChatGPT ya Gemini mein paste karo, topic badal lo):

> Mujhe compound interest samjhao. Ise real-life example se samjhao. Phir 3 practice sawal do, answers sabse last mein.

## Answers last mein kyun?

Roediger aur Karpicke (2006), Experiment 2: ek group ne ek passage 4 round padha. Doosre group ne 1 baar padha aur 3 baar khud ko test kiya. 5 minute baad padhne wale aage the (83% vs 71%). 1 hafte baad ulta: test walon ko 61% yaad tha, padhne walon ko 40%. Aur test walon ne passage lagbhag 3.4 baar padha tha, padhne walon ne 14.2 baar.

Isliye answers last mein: pehle khud try karo, phir check karo.

## Notebook mein check

- Rs 100, 10%, 2 saal: saal 1 Rs 110, saal 2 **Rs 121**. Simple interest: Rs 120.
- 10 saal ka snowball: compound Rs 259.37, simple Rs 200. Pehle 3 saal farak lagbhag zero, phir tezi se badhta hai.
- Practice sawal: Rs 200, 10%, 2 saal = Rs 242. Rs 1000, 5%, 2 saal = Rs 1102.5. Rs 500, 20%, 3 saal = Rs 864.

AI kabhi-kabhi hisaab mein galti karta hai. Uske answers calculator ya Python se check karo.
