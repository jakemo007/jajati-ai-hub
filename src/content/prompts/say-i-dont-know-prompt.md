## Kab use karein

Jab bhi tum AI se facts poochh rahe ho: numbers, dates, naam, rules, ya koi rare topic.

## Kaise use karein

Apna sawal likho, aur end mein ye line paste karo.

## Ye kaam kyun karta hai

OpenAI ki 2025 ki research ("Why Language Models Hallucinate") kehti hai ki training mein guess karne par number milte hain, aur "pata nahi" bolne par nahi. Bilkul exam jaisa: khaali chhodo to zero, guess karo to shayad marks. Ye line AI ko saaf permission deti hai ki "pata nahi" bolna theek hai.

Ye line galtiyan kam karti hai, khatam nahi. Zaroori baat hamesha khud check karo.
