## Kab use karein

Jab tumhare paas apna document hai (notes, office policy, product manual) aur tumhe usi ke basis par jawab chahiye.

## Kaise use karein

1. Ye line paste karo.
2. Uske neeche apna document ka text paste karo.
3. Last mein apna sawal likho.

## Ye RAG ka chhota version hai

Episode 4 mein RAG samjhaya hai: AI ko pehle sahi page do, phir jawab likhwao. Ye line wahi kaam haath se karti hai. Badi files ke liye NotebookLM jaisa tool better hai, jo source bhi dikhata hai.

Isse made-up answers kam hote hain, khatam nahi. Zaroori jawab document se khud match karo.

Office ki files kisi AI tool mein daalne se pehle company ki policy dekh lo.
