## Kab use karein

Jab office mein chhutti maangni ho aur email formal, short aur polite chahiye.

## Kaise use karein

1. Prompt copy karo, ChatGPT mein paste karo.
2. `[wajah]` ki jagah apni wajah likho. Din badalne ho to "2 din" bhi badlo.
3. Send karo. Jo email aaye, usme dates aur apna naam khud jodo.
4. Bhejne se pehle ek baar padh lo ki tone tumhare office ke hisaab se theek hai.

## Tip

Agar tumhare office ka email likhne ka ek fixed style hai, to prompt ke end mein likho "Is style mein likho:" aur apna purana email paste karo. Ye trick PROMPT sheet ka bonus trick 1 hai.
