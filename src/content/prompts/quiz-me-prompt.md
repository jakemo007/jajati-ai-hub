## Kab use karein

Padhne ke baad, revision ke time, ya exam se pehle.

## Kaise use karein

1. Jo padha hai wo paste karo, ya usi chat mein continue karo jahan AI ne samjhaya tha.
2. Ye line likho aur send karo.
3. AI ek sawal poochhega. Jawab do, phir agla.

"One at a time" zaroori hai. Iske bina AI teeno sawal ek saath de deta hai, aur answers bhi saath mein aa sakte hain.
