## Kab use karein

Research, assignment, office report, ya koi bhi kaam jahan facts galat nahi hone chahiye.

## Kaise use karein

1. Apna sawal likho, end mein ye line jodo.
2. Jawab aane ke baad **har link khud kholo** aur dekho ki wahan sach mein wahi likha hai.
3. Jo cheezein AI ne "verify nahi ho sakta" mark ki hain, unhe use mat karo jab tak khud check na kar lo.

## Dhyan rahe

AI fake links bhi bana sakta hai. AI se hi mat poochho "kya ye link sahi hai?", wo dobara guess karega. America mein ek case mein lawyers ne ChatGPT ke diye 6 fake cases court mein de diye the, aur unhone ChatGPT se hi poochha tha ki cases asli hain ya nahi. Court ne $5,000 ka fine lagaya (Mata v. Avianca, 2023).
