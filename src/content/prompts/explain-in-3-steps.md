## Kab use karein

Jab kuch samajhna ho: koi topic, koi news, koi lamba article.

## Kaise use karein

1. Jo samajhna hai wo paste karo (ya topic ka naam likho).
2. Uske neeche ye line likho.
3. Send karo. AI teacher ki tarah 3 steps mein samjhayega, ek real example ke saath.

Agla step: samajh aaya ya sirf padha? Uske liye "Quiz me" prompt use karo.
