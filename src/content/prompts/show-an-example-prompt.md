## Kab use karein

Jab tumhe ek khaas style chahiye: tumhara apna likhne ka tareeka, tumhari brand ki tone, ya office ka fixed format.

## Kaise use karein

1. Pehle apna normal prompt likho (Role, Context, Task, Format).
2. Uske end mein ye line jodo.
3. Bracket ki jagah wo example paste karo jiska style chahiye.

## Ye kaam kyun karta hai

Isse few-shot prompting kehte hain: AI ko ek example dikhao, aur wo usi pattern ko copy karta hai. OpenAI ki prompt engineering guide bhi examples dene ki salah deti hai.
