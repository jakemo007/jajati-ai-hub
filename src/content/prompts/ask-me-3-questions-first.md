## Kab use karein

Jab tumhe khud nahi pata ki prompt mein kya-kya batana hai. Trip plan, diet plan, gift idea, career advice: jahan jawab tumhari situation par depend karta hai.

## Kaise use karein

1. Apna normal sawal likho.
2. End mein ye line jodo: **Before you answer, ask me 3 questions.**
3. AI pehle 3 sawal poochhega. Unke jawab do.
4. Ab jo plan ya answer aayega, wo tumhari details par bana hoga.

## Ye kaam kyun karta hai

AI guess tab karta hai jab context missing ho. Ye line context ki zimmedari AI par daal deti hai: wo khud poochh leta hai ki use kya jaanna hai. Ye 4-part formula ka "Context" wala hissa bharne ka shortcut hai.
