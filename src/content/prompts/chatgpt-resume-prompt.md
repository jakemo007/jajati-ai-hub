## Kab use karein

Jab tumhare paas projects hain, par resume mein unhe kaise likhna hai ye samajh nahi aa raha. Ya jab tumhara resume ek lambi list ban gaya hai aur 1 page mein fit karna hai.

## Kaise use karein

1. Prompt copy karo aur ChatGPT mein paste karo.
2. "fresher, Python aur SQL, data analyst" ko apni detail se badlo: tumhara level, skills aur jo job chahiye.
3. `[yahan likho]` ki jagah apne projects likho. Har project ke liye: kya banaya, kaun se tools, aur result kya mila.
4. Send karo. Phir har line padho aur check karo ki sab sach hai.

## Dhyan rahe

- Wahi skills aur projects likho jo sach mein tumhare hain. AI kabhi-kabhi cheezein badha-chadha kar likh deta hai.
- Format part ("bullet points, har point 10 words se kam") hi resume ko chhota aur saaf rakhta hai. Use mat hatao.
