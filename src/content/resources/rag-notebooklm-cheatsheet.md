## RAG = AI ka open-book exam

1. **Chunk:** Files ko chhote tukdon mein kaato. Har tukda ek chunk.
2. **Embedding:** Har chunk ka meaning numbers mein. Ye numbers ek vector database mein rakhe jaate hain.
3. **Retrieval:** Sawal aaye to meaning se sabse milte-julte chunks dhoondho. Keyword se nahi.
4. **Answer:** Wo chunks prompt mein jodo. AI unhi se jawab deta hai, source ke saath.

## Free mein try karo: NotebookLM

- [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com/) kholo, Google account se sign in karo.
- Naya notebook banao aur source daalo: PDF, Google Doc, website link ya YouTube link.
- Sawal poochho. Jawab mein [1] jaise number par click karo, source ka wahi hissa dikhega.

## Dhyan rahe

- Galat chunk mila to jawab bhi galat.
- RAG jhooth kam karta hai, khatam nahi. Source khud check karo.
- Office ki files daalne se pehle company ki policy dekh lo.
