## Is sheet ko kaise use karein

1. Neeche apna prompt chuno, ya PDF download karke phone mein save kar lo.
2. **Copy prompt** dabao aur ChatGPT mein paste karo.
3. `[Bracket]` waali jagah apni detail bharo, aur brackets hata do.
4. Send karo. Jawab pasand na aaye, to ek line aur likho ki kya badalna hai.

Har prompt 4 cheezon par bana hai: **Role, Context, Task, Format.** Yahi formula AI Explained Episode 5 mein samjhaya gaya hai.
